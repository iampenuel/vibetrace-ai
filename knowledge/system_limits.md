# System Limits

Honest statements about what VibeTrace AI is and is not. The system is designed to
cite this document so that its own limitations travel alongside its recommendations.

## synthetic catalog
The song catalog is small and synthetic. Titles, artists, and audio-feature values
were invented for this classroom project. Recommendations describe fictional catalog
entries and say nothing reliable about real songs or real artists.

## educational context
The knowledge documents VibeTrace AI retrieves (genre, mood, energy, listening
contexts) are short educational notes written for this project. They provide general
background, not authoritative musicology, and were created specifically to be cited
as evidence.

## subjective quality
Recommendation quality is subjective. A high score means a song matched the requested
features well under a transparent formula, not that the listener will actually enjoy
it. Taste, mood, and context vary from person to person.

## evidence requirement
The system aims to make no substantive recommendation claim without retrieved
evidence. Every recommended song and explanation is tied to catalog records and,
when retrieval is enabled, to knowledge passages, each shown with an evidence
identifier.

## not spotify
VibeTrace AI is a small explainable prototype. It is not Spotify, not a production
recommendation model, and not trained on real listening data. It should not be
compared to commercial recommender systems in terms of scale or accuracy.

## no private history
The system has no access to real private listening history. It only uses the
optional synthetic sample profiles included with the project when a profile is
explicitly selected, and those profiles are fictional.

## no health claims
VibeTrace AI does not diagnose, treat, or make claims about mental, emotional, or
physical health. Mood labels such as "calm" or "happy" describe musical character,
not a listener's psychological state, and must never be read as medical advice.
