# Genre Guide

Neutral, educational descriptions of the genres represented in the VibeTrace AI
catalog. Genre labels are broad simplifications: a single track can blend several
genres, and two songs sharing a label can still sound very different.

## pop
Pop tends to have catchy melodies, clear vocals, and moderate-to-high energy.
Instrumentation is usually produced and vocal-forward. Common contexts include
casual listening, parties, and upbeat daytime activities. Limitation: "pop" spans
many sub-styles and energy levels, so the label alone is a weak predictor of feel.

## lofi
Lofi (low-fidelity) features relaxed beats, soft textures, and often instrumental
arrangements. Energy is usually low and acousticness moderate-to-high. It is
commonly used for studying, focus, and background listening. Limitation: lofi is
defined more by production aesthetic than by a strict musical structure.

## rock
Rock is guitar-driven with strong rhythms and frequently high energy. Instrumentation
centers on electric guitar, bass, and drums. Common contexts include workouts and
high-intensity listening. Limitation: rock ranges from mellow to aggressive.

## edm
Electronic dance music emphasizes synthesizers, strong beats, and high danceability.
Energy and tempo are typically high. Common contexts are workouts, dancing, and
parties. Limitation: EDM covers many sub-genres with different intensities.

## ambient
Ambient music prioritizes atmosphere and texture over rhythm. Energy is usually very
low and acousticness or instrumentalness high. Common contexts include relaxation,
sleep, and reflective listening. Limitation: ambient can feel too sparse for active tasks.

## jazz
Jazz features improvisation, swing or relaxed rhythms, and acoustic instrumentation
such as piano, saxophone, and upright bass. Energy is often low-to-moderate. Common
contexts include relaxing, dining, and reflective evenings. Limitation: jazz spans
gentle standards to intense experimental styles.

## hip hop
Hip hop centers on rhythmic vocals (rap), strong beats, and higher speechiness.
Energy is moderate-to-high. Common contexts include casual listening and workouts.
Limitation: hip hop themes and intensity vary widely, and some tracks are explicit.

## classical
Classical music uses orchestral or solo acoustic instrumentation with wide dynamic
range. Energy is often low-to-moderate and acousticness very high. Common contexts
include focus, relaxation, and reflective listening. Limitation: classical spans
calm pieces to dramatic, high-energy works.

## folk
Folk emphasizes acoustic guitar, storytelling vocals, and gentle rhythms. Energy is
usually low-to-moderate. Common contexts include relaxing and reflective listening.
Limitation: the label covers many regional and modern styles.

## synthwave
Synthwave uses retro synthesizers and steady mid-tempo beats with a moody, nostalgic
feel. Energy is moderate. Common contexts include night driving and focused, moody
listening. Limitation: it is a stylistic aesthetic more than a strict genre.

## metal
Metal features distorted guitars, fast tempos, and very high energy. Common contexts
include intense workouts and high-adrenaline listening. Limitation: metal has many
sub-genres and is often explicit or aggressive, which does not suit calm settings.

## country
Country blends acoustic and electric instrumentation with narrative vocals and
moderate energy. Common contexts include relaxed and social listening. Limitation:
modern country overlaps heavily with pop and rock.

## r&b
R&B (rhythm and blues) emphasizes smooth vocals, groove, and romantic or reflective
moods at moderate energy. Common contexts include relaxing and evening listening.
Limitation: R&B ranges from mellow ballads to upbeat dance tracks.

## soul
Soul features expressive vocals, warm instrumentation, and uplifting or heartfelt
moods at moderate energy. Common contexts include relaxed and reflective listening.
Limitation: overlaps with R&B and funk.

## reggae
Reggae uses off-beat rhythms, relaxed grooves, and generally happy or laid-back moods
at moderate energy. Common contexts include relaxing and social settings. Limitation:
sub-styles vary in tempo and intensity.

## indie pop
Indie pop combines pop melodies with a more understated, alternative production.
Energy is moderate-to-high. Common contexts include casual and upbeat listening.
Limitation: "indie" describes an ethos as much as a sound.
