VibeTrace AI — Intent Classifier Specialization Experiment
==============================================================

Setup: deterministic 4-fold stratified cross-validation
(seed=42) over a hand-written synthetic dataset of 192 examples
across 8 intents. Every example is scored while held out of training.
This is a small classroom specialization, NOT a production language model.

Overall cross-validated accuracy
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Keyword baseline        :  58.85%
  Specialized classifier  :  74.48%
  Improvement             : +15.62 points

Per-intent cross-validated accuracy (baseline -> model)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  discover       support=24   95.83% ->  66.67%
  study          support=24   58.33% ->  45.83%
  workout        support=24   58.33% ->  58.33%
  relax          support=24   50.00% ->  87.50%
  compare        support=24   58.33% -> 100.00%
  explain        support=24   50.00% ->  95.83%
  diversify      support=24   50.00% ->  91.67%
  out_of_scope   support=24   50.00% ->  50.00%

Interpretation: the trained model generalizes to paraphrases the
keyword rules miss (e.g. novel wording for 'study' or 'relax'), while
the baseline only fires on exact keywords. Both are transparent.
